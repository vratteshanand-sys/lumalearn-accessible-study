# LumaLearn — separate post drafts

These drafts describe the current prototype and verified work. They have not been posted to social media.

## LinkedIn

Sometimes the hardest part of studying is getting started.

LumaLearn is a small study companion built around a short reading lesson, read-aloud support, comfortable text options and a focus timer.

I have published the existing project on Base44 and worked on a few issues that made the lesson less helpful. Learners can now retry after a wrong answer, progress updates no longer reset the lesson step, and saved progress is checked before loading.

I verified the published flow from a wrong answer through correction and completion, then reloaded the page to check that progress remained saved.

It is an early prototype with one lesson and progress stored on the current device. My aim is to make that small experience useful before adding more.

Try it: https://luma-learn-accessible-study-3704b4c4.base44.app/
